## Design Context

### Users
Turkish lawyers preparing contracts, commercial disputes and employment matters,
with curators reviewing source evidence before legal corpus publication. Users
need exact document passages, uncertainty and accountable review decisions.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, accountable. This is an implementation direction for the existing
workbench, drawn from the user's emphasis on grounded legal preparation and review.

### Aesthetic Direction
Continue the current restrained editorial workspace and local fonts. Preserve the
user-requested left navigation, middle work area and right contextual assistant,
with collapsible, adjustable and full-screen panels. No global visual redesign is
part of this development packet. Keep technical details in collapsible disclosures.

### Design Principles
- Put exact evidence beside the action that depends on it.
- Separate proposals, accepted reviews, stale work and publication status.
- Preserve unknown information and expose incomplete coverage.
- Require confirmed server results before showing a saved legal-review decision.
- Reuse established controls and support keyboard and narrow-panel operation.
