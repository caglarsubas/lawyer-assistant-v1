## Bounded review session

Keep real originals, personal information, reviewer names and review notes in
access-controlled operational or authorized matter storage. Do not put them in
GitHub, this checklist, the HTML roadmap or a provider prompt.

| Review | Inspect | Required recorded outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Source rights |Exact representation/hash, route, audience, expiry and uses, including storage/indexing/display/inference/export/offline distribution |Attributable use-specific decision with supporting evidence; unsupported uses stay disabled |
| Text fidelity |Original versus independently transcribed passages; tables, footnotes, negation, dates, identifiers and amounts |Discrepancies, incomplete extraction, measured reviewer minutes and disagreements |
| Norm history |Enacted/consolidated distinction, amendment chain, transitional provisions and historical versions |Exact provision/version links, unresolved citations and defensible validity cutoffs |
| Graph A semantics |Entity/relationship catalog and conditional competence, institution and procedure examples |Independent semantic decision; distinguish hierarchy, appellate route, territory and normative authority |
| Graph B semantics |Proceeding versus decision; allegation versus finding; majority versus dissent; citation versus treatment/effect |Evidence-backed identity/treatment decisions and disagreements; no effect inferred from citation alone |
| Private work product |Facts, allegations, issues, supporting/adverse evidence, premises/rules/application and limitations |Lawyer corrections and exact independent review decisions; unsupported applications remain qualified or withheld |

review process; it does not meet those targets or national coverage. Reviewers
must ratify sample minima, family splits and independent adjudication before
held-out evaluation. Keep observed totals and denominators separate from targets.
