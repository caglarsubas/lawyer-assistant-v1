# Private research source nominations

Research runs now collect independent released source passages alongside graph
paths and ordinary authority candidates. Each result remains in the encrypted,
authorized matter product. Source selections never create public inverse links,
cross-matter knowledge, external queries or source index mutations.

The research screen shows a separate source inspection panel with exact quoted
text, source/version identifiers, locators, hashes and explicit unknown effects.
Text remains inert. Coverage distinguishes scan and result limits from national
completeness, and no-hit results do not imply absence of relevant law.

Before reuse, saved source snapshots must match the exact current release,
serving/ontology hashes and activation sequence. Each saved hit is re-projected
from signed source RDF; identities, quote bytes, hashes, offsets and uncertainty
fields must match exactly. Changed metadata, duplicated identities or invented
authority/finality/applicability labels withhold the work. Even zero-hit source
captures retain their publication dependency. Legacy document-only records remain
readable without acquiring an invented public-corpus dependency.

Current permission failure projects the work as stale and withholds public source
hits, ordinary authority hits and graph paths. The stored encrypted version and
authorized private evidence remain intact. Display is not a repair, fresh review,
or source approval. Product reads use `Cache-Control: no-store`; membership is
checked again before returning the product. Review and export remain guarded.

DOCX/PDF exports include source quotes, locators, version/release identities,
source/text/locator-map/quote hashes, offsets and coverage limitations. The same
current guard revalidates source grounding before rendering and at exit.
Presentation normalizes CRLF/CR line endings to LF, and PDF preserves explicit
line breaks; original UTF-8 quote hashes and offsets address the unmodified source
record. Pagination can reflow text, so exported layout is not a byte-identical
copy of the original source representation.

Public nominations are not inserted into the private-document inference envelope
or generated claims. Claims remain validated private document quotations, and
`legal_corpus_unqualified` remains a critical gap. Source availability alone does
not establish a completed legal analysis or lawyer qualification benchmark.

A completed job record precedes the publication guard's exit and independent
authorization callback. Pending revalidation continues to withhold public results
and block exports; commit completion must not be mistaken for serving permission.
Tests wait for the callback independently rather than weakening this boundary.

Qualification uses invented signed sources, actual guarded research persistence,
DOCX/PDF inspection, source withdrawal, changed retained fields, zero-hit pin
checks, private model envelopes and inert UI rendering. Real licensed population,
legal/editorial approval, deployment and lawyer-adjudicated usefulness remain
open. Next: typed evidence lookup and reproducible comparison of independent
source discovery with graph-assisted authority retrieval, including explicit
adverse/unresolved authority and coverage gaps.
