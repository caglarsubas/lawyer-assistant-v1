# Complete retained mixed Graph A/B assembly

The offline assembler can extend an installed, approved mixed release. Keep the
closed `legal-declared-graph-selection-v1` declaration: `expected_base` is the
exact observed active pointer, `structure` nominates a reviewed structural
component, and `jurisprudence` nominates one to eight reviewed decision components.
The nominated components are additions to the whole retained base. Omitting an
earlier component from this new list never removes its data or dependencies.

Use the existing commands in [declared assembly](DECLARED_GRAPH_ASSEMBLY.md),
[final candidates](FINAL_GRAPH_CANDIDATES.md), and
[whole-release authorization](WHOLE_RELEASE_AUTHORIZATION.md). The complete new
assembly needs a fresh external fourth-key final attestation and a fresh external
fifth-key whole-release permission. No command generates keys or signatures.
Prior permission cannot authorize a successor, and successor permission cannot
outlive any retained component or parent whole-release permission.

Every prior installed release must exactly match its original signed public
candidate. Retain that candidate, its sealed original final-review packet, its
accepted whole-release permission, and all original structural/decision
preparations and grants at their vetted paths. Neither a public bundle nor an
inventory copied to a new location can stand in for these dependencies.

Discovery verifies the original external grants, sealed private bindings, and
complete public payloads without opening nested source transactions. The union
plan then locks all accounts, sources, mappings, canonical reservations and review
contexts in global order in one original-source transaction. Each original
structural source set and each independently reviewed decision context is checked
against its original preparation and current rights, identities, equivalence,
institutional-register evidence, reviewer appointment, keys and epoch. Exact
duplicated physical components and source selections are deduplicated only within
this operation. Conflicting pins, grant/path aliases, source-family collisions,
or implicit shared-identity redefinitions fail closed.

The assembler reconstructs every earlier assembly and its exact signed dependency
fingerprint. It preserves all original triples, public source manifestations,
physical evidence, and earlier signed inventory nodes. A new inventory lists the
complete leaf components and records the whole frozen immediate base. Its hash is
inside the newly attested Graph A. No new relationship, competence, legal effect,
institutional alias, finality, or unknown date is inferred from retention.

The frozen original base remains a dependency after activation and rollback.
Current serving guards continue to check the exact live before/after pointer,
held lock and monotonic CAS separately. Any revoked or expired retained dependency
withholds the descendant release as well as the original. No prior permission
verdict is reused on a subsequent operation. Exit checks re-read all original
ledgers, public/private bytes, inventories, trust files and current lifetimes;
failed preparation discards only its newly owned artifact.

The operation rejects a retained cycle, more than four mixed ancestors, more than
32 distinct leaf components, eight distinct structural source selections, 32
decision contexts, 64 distinct source identities, 2,048 distinct physical files,
or 256 MiB of retained dependencies. Physical file/byte budgets are charged before
recursive decoding or RDF validation. Existing per-packet, original-source,
private-snapshot, and 64 MiB output limits also apply. These bounds never truncate
an assembly. Larger inventories require a separately designed and qualified
workflow; they do not authorize dropping an ancestor.

Engineering tests use invented sources and TEST-ONLY signatures. They cover new
structural and decision sources, a shared register, successive mixed releases,
whole-byte preservation, independent successor permission, rollback, source/key/
epoch/grant changes, complete budgets and real concurrent PostgreSQL readers.
They do not establish national historical corpus completeness, legal correctness,
production signing, deployment or lawyer acceptance. Private matter overlays
remain outside shared Graph A/B releases.

Use [read-only dependency inspection](RETAINED_DEPENDENCY_INSPECTION.md) to
observe the exact recorded inventory separately from present conjunctive
permission. Its private receipt authorizes no publication, activation or retry.
