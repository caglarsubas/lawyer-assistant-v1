# Retirement and rollback recovery qualification

This separate frozen campaign extends the existing joint-recovery controller's
renewal evidence to retirement and rollback. No new runtime route is registered,
real operator enrolled or private case migrated.

## Retirement

A fresh independently reviewed window names the retirement. Its destination
retirement event and exact finish can be reconciled one anchor at a time, with
the destination preceding the finish. Retirement never creates another namespace
transition, falls back to the legacy reader or makes the retired selection
available again. Successful completion metadata is distinct from inventory
permission: ordinary reads remain denied after retirement.

## Rollback

An earlier selection is an exact historical target, not a reusable approval.
Rollback creates a fresh selection with a fresh child decision and a fresh
namespace transition. It binds the target, stage, receipt, current source and
reviewed window. Reusing a prior namespace decision cannot open that window.
Old cursors remain invalid after the fresh rollback succeeds.
